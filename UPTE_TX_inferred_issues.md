# UPTE TX inferred contract: issues

**Inferred draft.** All 16 packet documents have been reviewed, but the
successor agreement cannot be fully reconstructed from the identified sources.
This is not an executed contract and must not be relied on as a complete
successor agreement.

## ACC-01: Accreted-worker orientation

The successor MOU, section 11.a (packet PDF page 3), specifies a 30-minute,
unpaid union orientation for accreted workers and refers to a mediation TA.
Document 12's HX access proposal does not contain a separate accreted-worker
orientation clause. The detailed application and relationship to the
existing Article 1 orientation provisions remain unresolved. The MOU wording
is retained in Article 1 with an issue marker.

## UBL-01: Short-term union business leave

The successor MOU, section 13.b (packet PDF page 3), reduces the short-term
notice period from 30 to 20 days and refers to a UC proposal dated October 17,
2024. The complete referenced proposal is not separately identified among the
16 split packet documents. Article 17 retains the existing provision with the
notice period changed, subject to reconciliation if the complete proposal is
found in the supplied materials.

## ACC-02: Representative formula

Section 11.b of the MOU eliminates the location cap and states a formula of
adding a representative for each additional 100 unit employees, as well as
12 hours per month per representative. Article 1 removes the former cap and
changes the time references, reproduces the MOU formula, and retains the former
threshold and departmental provisions pending the complete applicable TX
implementing text. Document 12's HX proposal includes alternate-representative
and departmental language, but the MOU does not explicitly extend those
details to TX.

## COMP-01: Compensation sources and historical provisions

The MOU, section 1 (packet PDF pages 1–2), refers to the “UPTE Market Based
Adj Mediation Proposal 10-29-25,” “UPTE Equity Pool Allocation 110425,” and
UC's LBFO+ (10/29 7PM). These are not separately identified among the 16
split packet documents. Their precise scales, allocations, and step
structures cannot be inferred. The MOU text is retained in Article 6, but its
relationship to the former contract's historical wage and LBNL provisions
requires reconciliation. Its LBNL terms expressly require reasonable efforts
to secure approval; they should not be treated as an unconditional award.
The MOU's reference to section 1.b.2 is retained as printed.

## DEV-01: Training and professional development

The MOU, section 10 (packet PDF pages 2–3), summarizes paid leave on scheduled
days off, remote use of all hours, and travel/lodging reimbursement, and
refers to a UC proposal dated November 8, 2024. The summarized terms are
retained in Article 8. Their complete implementing language and relationship
to the existing Article 8 provisions, including LBNL-specific restrictions,
are not yet established.

## DUR-01: Successor duration

The MOU heading gives November 7, 2025 through September 30, 2028, while its
signatures are dated November 8, 2025. The former Article 9 dates have been
preserved with a marker rather than silently replaced. The complete successor
duration language, including any precise effective time, has not been
identified.

## ACR-01: Accretion terms and internal reference

The MOU section 12 describes status quo protections, a pilot dispute process
and protection of vacation/PTO accruals. The available wording is retained in
Article 2 and cross-referenced in Article 43. The MOU references titles
identified in “13.c,” but section 13 has no subsection c; no correction is
assumed. Section 12.c refers to packet document 06. Its MSP protection is
limited to specified pre-accretion MSP incumbents and their current titles,
whereas the MOU summarizes a broader protection of incumbents' vacation/PTO
accruals; the relationship is unresolved. Document 06 labels eight titles TX,
labels others HX, and does not specify the unit for the two added infection
prevention coordinator titles.

## HLT-01: Healthcare baseline and historical terms

The healthcare counterproposal in document 02 is retained in Article 4,
including all monthly subsidy values. It references the University's 2026
rates as the baseline, but does not state those rates or define the pay
bands. It provides percentage caps beginning thereafter; the former Article
4 has historical premium provisions including a $25 cap. Their precise
relationship is not spelled out in the counterproposal. Appendices D and F
are historical; Appendix F's 2014 figures are not 2026 rates.

## RECL-01: Reclassification mirroring

MOU section 4 directs RX/TX to mirror the attached HX reclassification
changes. Document 05 is headed RX/TX/HX and Article 41 (RX/TX); its marked
changes are incorporated in Article 41. Its unmarked Staff Research Associate
I provision is not imported as a new TX entitlement merely because it appears
in the shared draft. The source skips from E.1.f to E.1.h after that provision.
The source's upward-reclassification increase uses “whichever is greater”
and expressly caps discretionary additional increases; the former TX wording
also capped the four-percent increase. The relationship requires confirmation.

## RECL-02: Hiring and seniority conflicts

Document 05 adds consideration of substantially equally qualified applicants,
whereas the former TX article says to “first consider” career employees. Both
are retained pending clarification rather than silently deleting the earlier
term. Document 05 cites Article 25, Section B, for nondiscrimination; in the
former TX article Section B defines sexual harassment. Its Article 41 seniority
and tie-breaker provisions use date of hire and pay-status hours, whereas
Article 16 uses full-time-equivalent service and date of appointment in the
reverse order. The source does not identify which rule governs a conflict.

## RECL-03: Classification meetings addendum

Document 05 calls the classification-meetings addendum “Article 46 – Workplace
Committees” and ends with “Article 16 insert management rights article.”
TX Article 46 is Work Rules, Article 15 covers labor-management meetings and
Article 19 is Management Rights. The exact source wording, including “up 4
times,” is provisionally placed in Article 15; its final location, meaning
and relationship to existing meetings and rights have not been determined.

## RECL-04: Prior classification-review steps

The former TX article allows forwarding when a supervisor fails to respond
within 30 calendar days and directs the University office's response to the
supervisor with a copy to the employee. Document 05's new acknowledgment,
forwarding and written-results provisions use different actors and recipients.
The former wording is retained alongside the new text pending reconciliation.

## REM-01: Remote-work notice and LBNL

Document 06's remote-work section says changes require 30-day written notice
but also addresses notice when 30 days is not possible and says a minimum
30 days shall be provided for schedules established at hire. Its precise
interaction is not resolved. Existing LBNL telecommuting language permits
termination at any time and says 30 days' notice should be given when
possible. Both sets of text are retained in Article 13 pending reconciliation.

## LAY-02: Layoff numbering and cross-references

Document 03 inserts “C. Alternative to Layoff,” labels both temporary and
indefinite layoff sections D, and retains references such as “Section C.1.”
for temporary layoff notice. It numbers indefinite layoff selection as 1,
but then skips to 3 for notice, and labels LBNL re-employment as 3 after
severance 1. The existing split article used the former numbering. The new
alternative section is identified explicitly, while existing labels and
cross-references are retained rather than guessing a comprehensive renumbering.

## LAY-03: Layoff source wording

Document 03 includes a period after “special skills” before “is essential” in
the new non-career employee provision. Its severance alternatives use the
phrase “in lieu of preferential rehire and recall rights with reduced
severance,” and LBNL voluntary severance says the employee “will” be provided
a payment while the next sentence still discusses when approval is normally
given. The LBNL cross-location provision conditions termination of recall
rights on passing a trial period while specifying the date of hiring as the
termination date. These source readings are retained without deciding the
parties' intended meaning.

## LAY-04: Related layoff provisions

Document 03 deletes an express exclusion of term appointees in the LBNL
severance section and states that non-probationary career employees are
eligible. Article 31 separately says Article 16's layoff and severance
provisions do not apply to term employees. Their relationship has not been
resolved. Article 40 separately specifies a subcontracting-displacement
alternative and says its provisions control when inconsistent with Article
16; document 03 supplies no express amendment to Article 40.

## HOL-01: Holiday proposal and Cesar Chavez exception

MOU section 7 provides an additional floating holiday and a Cesar Chavez
exception to the cited holiday proposals. The source wording is retained in
Article 12. The RX/TX proposal in document 09 lists Cesar Chavez as an
administrative holiday, with a special LBNL floating-holiday rule, while the
MOU says “Cesar Chavez remains as floating holiday.” The MOU does not itself
spell out how that exception modifies the general list, the existing LBNL
rule, or the separately stated additional floating holiday. Both known
wordings are retained with a marker.

## HOL-02: Exempt and non-exempt holiday pay

Document 09 inserts exempt-employee holiday-not-worked rules. Its corresponding
non-exempt full-time clause says both “eight (8) hours” and “equal to the
employee’s regular shift” regardless of shift length. The prior TX clause
provides eight hours without that qualification and does not distinguish
exempt status. The new exempt text and both non-exempt readings are retained
in Article 12. Document 09 also says “A non-exempt employee” for the
time-and-one-half worked-holiday provision, while the former TX article says
“An employee”; the former text remains pending clarification because the
difference is not visibly marked as a change.

## VAC-01: Vacation proposals and later language

MOU section 8 refers to UC's August 15 and 16, 2024 proposals with additional
language dated November 7 and 8, 2024. The RX/TX proposal appears in packet
document 07 and its redlines are incorporated in Article 43; the separate
additional language has not been identified. Document 07 strikes an A&PS
accrual provision dated November 1, 1997, while the corresponding former TX
provision is dated September 1, 1997. The TX counterpart is removed as the
identified struck provision, subject to reconciliation with the missing later
language and the distinct document 06 MSP-incumbent protection.

## VAC-02: Scheduling and seniority

Document 07 adds a ten-business-day decision requirement for written
requests and an endeavor to respond to “additional vacation request(s)”
within fifteen calendar days, without defining the latter or their
relationship. It also introduces seniority-based scheduling when a
department establishes a vacation schedule, without identifying which
seniority definition applies; Articles 16 and 41 contain different rules.

## LEA-01: Pregnancy and childcaring leave

MOU section 9 incorporates NX Article 20(D) and extends later improvements
within 120 days of CNA ratification. Article 18 reproduces the complete
document 11 excerpt alongside the MOU and former TX leave provisions. The
source repeatedly says “nurse” and references NX sections B and C that are
outside the excerpt; the MOU extends the provisions to UPTE units, but no
silent adaptation or TX cross-reference mapping is assumed. The source
includes “up to) four (4) months” and a twelve-month combined cap; the
punctuation and interaction with the former TX parental, pregnancy and
disability leave provisions are unresolved. Later NX improvements and their
ratification are not identified in this packet.

## SEN-01: Seniority side letter

MOU section 17 refers to UC Proposal #2 of March 10, 2025 for incorporation
in the appropriate article. Its full text has not been identified among the
split packet documents. Articles 16 and 41 contain seniority-related terms,
but their final relationship to the side letter is not established.

## SCK-01, GRV-01 and ARB-01: Earlier proposals

The MOU refers to the sick-leave proposals of August 15 and 16, 2024; UC's
last grievance proposals of November 7, 2024; and UC's last arbitration
proposals of August 28, except the arbitrators' list. These complete
proposals have not been identified. Articles 39, 10 and 3 are marked
provisional. The MOU says remaining arbitrator-list disputes shall be
negotiated by counsel no later than 30 days after ratification; the outcome is
not known from the MOU.

## TIT-01: Accreted title terms and TX applicability

MOU section 15 states that Dietitian 4 scales shall mirror Dietitian Sr
at the respective campuses, with the same number of steps and each step 10%
above the corresponding Dietitian Sr step; it also references a BHC/BHPP
proposal passed November 5 at 3:54 PM and Optoms proposals passed November 6
at 1:22 AM. The MOU does not itself identify the units for these titles.
Documents 14 and 15 contain wage tables whose relationship to these
references and applicability to TX are not established; title-specific
terms are not represented as TX entitlements solely on this basis.
The exact MOU title-specific wording is reproduced in Article 6 as uncertain
source material.

## OPT-01: Optometrist scale and placement uncertainty

Document 14 supplies Optometrist 3 and 4 scales and initial-placement rules,
but does not state their bargaining units. Document 06 identifies Optometrist
4 as HX; document 14 does not establish a TX extension. The unit of
Optometrist 3 is not identified in these materials. Its scale and the
placement language are preserved as candidate source text in Appendix A,
not asserted as TX rights. The MOU references a November 6 Optoms proposal,
whereas document 14 is dated November 5, so their exact relationship is
unclear. Placement brackets also use “less than” and “more than” at three,
six and ten years, leaving exact boundaries unstated, and mix relevant
experience with UC experience; no missing boundaries are invented.

## BHC-01: Behavioral-health scales and unit uncertainty

Document 15 contains counselor levels 1–4 and behavioral-health psychological
professional levels 1–4, with eight location/step tables. It does not label
the units or state the time of the MOU's November 5 3:54 PM proposal. Document
06 labels “BEH HEALTH PSYCHTRC PROFL 4” HX, but does not establish whether
that title is identical to document 15's “BEH HEALTH PSYCH PROFL 4,” code
5726. All eight tables are preserved in Appendix A as candidate source data,
with no representation that they establish TX rights. Empty cells remain
empty rather than being inferred.

## PA-01 and PTO-01: HX-specific side letters

MOU sections 14 and 16 reference a physician assistant side letter and
removal of the UCLA PTO side letter. Packet documents 13 and 16 identify
their subject provisions as HX-specific. The MOU does not explicitly extend
those HX terms to TX; they are tracked here rather than represented as TX
contract rights. Document 13 is expressly an HX side letter applicable to
medical centers only. Document 16 conditions the UCLA conversion on
ratification of the HX successor agreement and references HX Articles 36 and
42. Its PDF shows blank signature lines; the split-file description's
assertion that it is signed is not supported by that page.

## Baseline extraction

The 53 split files match consecutive sections of the former contract's
Markdown source, except that the Article 36 heading `AR TIC L E 36` was changed
to `ARTICLE36` and trailing whitespace differs. No substantive difference
was detected by that exact-text comparison; it does not verify the former PDF
or establish that its Markdown extraction is complete.
