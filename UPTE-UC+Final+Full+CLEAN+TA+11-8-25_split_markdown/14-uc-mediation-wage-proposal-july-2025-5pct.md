The University rates are presented as an hourly rate, but based upon FLSA status the position will be compensated hourly or annual salary.

These guidelines only apply to the initial step placement for current Optometrists who are accreted and moved into the new step structure. Relevant work experience is defined as
time worked at the equivalent classification level; experience earned in technician or other non-Optometrist roles does not count toward qualifying experience.
Employees with less than three years of relevant work experience in the classification listed above shall be placed on Step 1 of the appropriate salary structure or the step closest to
their current pay, whichever is higher.
Employees with more than three years of relevant work experience and less than six years of UC experience in the classification listed above shall be guaranteed to be placed on no
lower than Step 2 of the appropriate salary structure or the step closest to their current pay, whichever is higher.
Employees with more than six years of relevant work experience and less than ten years of UC experience in the classification listed above shall be guaranteed to be placed on no
lower than Step 3 of the appropriate salary structure or the step closest to their current pay, whichever is higher.
Employees with more than ten years of relevant work experience shall be guaranteed to be placed on no lower than Step 4 of the appropriate salary structure or the step closest to
their current pay, whichever is higher.
