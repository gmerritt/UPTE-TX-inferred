                                       APPENDIX XX
                             PHYSICIAN ASSISTANT SIDE LETTER
                          (APPLICABLE TO MEDICAL CENTERS ONLY)

   A. GENERAL PROVISIONS

          1. Unless otherwise noted, "Physician Assistant" in this side letter shall include all
             job classifications in the Physician Assistant series and “Nurse Practitioner”
             includes all job classifications in the Nurse Practitioner series.

   B. COMPENSATION

          1. The University shall adjust wage scales between the Physician Assistants as
             described in the attached appendices.

                 a.   The University and UPTE shall locally bargain reasonable title and step
                      placement of Physician Assistants. No PA or PA Sr shall face a
                      reduction in pay.

          2. The University will create a Physician Assistant 3 position in accordance with the
             attached Physician Assistant series concept.

                 a. The University shall locally bargain a reasonable promotion process from
                    Physician Assistant 2 to Physician Assistant 3.

          3.   For the purposes of this side letter:
                 a. Physician Assistant 1 shall be aligned with Nurse Practitioner 1;
                 b. Physician Assistant 2 shall be aligned with Nurse Practitioner 2;
                 c. Physician Assistant 3 shall be aligned with Nurse Practitioner 3; and
                 d. Physician Assistant Per Diem shall be aligned with Nurse Practitioner Per
                     Diem.
                 Physician Assistant 1s shall advance to Physician Assistant 2, contingent
                 upon the completion of six (6) months of satisfactory performance.

          4.   The University shall make the above adjustments within 90 days of resolving the
               above-disputed items.

          5.   Any prior experience in work as a physician assistant, post-graduate
               fellowships, and/or certificates (CAQ) will apply towards step placement.
