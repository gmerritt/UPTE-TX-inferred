UPTE Healthcare Counterproposal
             11/6/25 10PM

             Health Care:

                 1. The University's 2026 rates would serve as the baseline for rates moving forward.
                 2. Every year thereafter, UPTE employees would have a 5% cap on the University’s
                    Blue and Gold medical premium rate increases and a 7.5% cap on the University’s
                    Kaiser premium rate increases.
                 3. UPTE employees would have the following monthly subsidies.
                 4. In no event will subsidies cause net employee premium costs to fall below rates for
                    UPTE employees in the previous year.
                 5. If UC offers a lower percentage cap for Kaiser or Blue and Gold or more favorable
                    tapering of subsidies to EX or NX units, the same will be offered to UPTE. The “me
                    too” does not apply to the overall subsidy dollar rate or to the overall cost.
                 6. Following expiration of the CBA, if the parties have not reached an overall
                    agreement for a successor contract, UC may increase the premium rates subject to
                    the caps above, provided that subsidies continue. The parties agree to arbitrate any
                    disputes over the application of this language pursuant to the Grievance and
                    Arbitration articles, and that the Arbitration article will remain in effect after
                    contract expiration as to such disputes.
