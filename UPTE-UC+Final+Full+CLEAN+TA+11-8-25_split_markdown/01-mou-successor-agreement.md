Memorandum of Understanding
                            Successor Agreement Between UPTE and the University of California
                                        November 7, 2025 - September 30, 2028

                 1. Compensation
                      a. Across-The-Board (ATB) raises
                             i.  5% in July 2025, 5% in July 2026, 4% in July 2027 and 4% in July 2028
                      b. 2025 Title-specific Equity Increases
                             i.  UC market equity increases in “UPTE Market Based Adj Mediation
                                 Proposal 10-29-25”
                            ii.  All others listed by title in “UPTE Equity Pool Allocation 110425,”
                                 (provided on 11/4/25 at 4:45pm) to be provided to all workers except
                                 those whose title receive an equity adjustment in 1.b.i. above. UC must
                                 raise any objections to UPTE's allocations by COB 11/10/2025.
                           iii.  Title-specific equity to be provided within 120 days of ratification;
                                 retroactive to first pay period following ratification
                          iv.    Total allocation of title-specific equity increases not to exceed value of 2%
                                 ATB. If the total increases described in 1.b.i and 1.b.2 equal greater than
                                 the value of a 2% ATB then the increases described in 1.b.2 will be adjusted
                                 until the total is equal to 2% ATB.
                      c. Impacts of new $25 minimum wage
                             i.  All workers on steps below $25/hr moved to at least $25/hr as of July 1,
                                 2025
                            ii.  All steps below $25 removed from scales within 120 days of ratification
                                 and workers placed on step 1 of scale retroactive to date ratification
                          iii.   Any title-specific increase from 1.b. above will be applied to workers
                                 impacted by 1.c.i after placement on step one above in 1.c.ii.
                          iv.    Workers impacted by 1.c.i above receive base-building step increases of
                                 at least 2% in January 2026 and thereafter unless they reach the top of
                                 the applicable step
                            v.   One additional step will be added to affected scales every July starting in
                                 2026 through the life of the contract
                      d. Step Increases for all UPTE-represented employees outside LBNL
                             i.  Paid in January of each year of the contract
                            ii.  Lump sums (pensionable but not base building) for those at top step in
                                 each year of the contract - as well as retroactive to January 2025 for
                                 those who did not have a step increase imposed retroactive to January
                                 2025 except as noted below in 1.d.iv
                          iii.   Steps added to structures for title series per UC’s LBFO+ (10/29 7PM)
                          iv.    All workers who would have been eligible for new steps negotiated in this
                                 agreement (in UC’s LBFO+ and UCLA scales formerly on experience
                                 based steps) and who did not have a step increase imposed retroactive to
                                 January 2025 shall receive a step increase in January 2026
