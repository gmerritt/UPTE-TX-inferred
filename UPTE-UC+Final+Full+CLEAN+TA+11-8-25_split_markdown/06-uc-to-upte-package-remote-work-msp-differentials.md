   Remote Work

   1. The University supports the use of hybrid and remote work agreements when
      operationally feasible to support work-life balance, reduce the burden and
      environmental impact of commuting, and to maximize the efficient use of space in
      University facilities, among other benefits.
   2. The parties acknowledge that the University has the management right to determine
      whether work is performed on-site, remotely, or in a hybrid format. The University’s
      decisions regarding approval, denial or modification of remote/hybrid arrangements
      are not subject to grievance or arbitration.
   3. Remote and Hybrid agreements are subject to each location’s approval process.
      (Schedules for part-time roles will be determined by the department in alignment
      with this general framework.)
          1. Exceptions are subject to each location’s approval process.
   4. Remote and hybrid work agreements shall be approved or denied by location’s
      approval process.
   5. A formal agreement is not required when an employee and supervisor agree to a
      non-precedent setting, ad hoc or rare occasion work schedule adjustment.
   6. Any changes to remote/hybrid work agreements will require 30-day written notice to
      the affected individual and the union. This does not include ad-hoc or rare occasion
      adjustments. Should 30 day notice not be possible, the University agrees to meet
      and confer regarding the shortened notice period as soon as practicable, upon
      receipt of a request to meet from UPTE. The University shall provide a minimum 30
      days written notice but shall strive to provide a 60 days written notice when a hybrid
      or remote schedule was established at the time of hire.
   7. Existing employee disability accommodations remain in effect and will continue to
      follow the review cadence.
   8. Management will provide work stations/site for the onsite work days. Items that
      should be considered include but are not limited to adequate desk/office space,
      and appropriate patient care areas that meet patient privacy considerations.
   9. Procedural disputes over the terms of this agreement shall be subject to available
      procedures outlined in the CBA.

Differentials:
Nothing in this article prohibits the parties from meeting and conferring upon mutual
agreement regarding the use of shift differentials at particular locations. The Campus or
Medical Centers shall ensure that relevant decision makers from the respective
department or location are consulted in shift differential bargaining.
