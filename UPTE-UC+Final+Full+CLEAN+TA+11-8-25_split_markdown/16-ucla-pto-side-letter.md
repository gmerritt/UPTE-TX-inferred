UCLA PTO SIDE LETTER
8.29.2024
University Proposal No. 1

                      Side letter PTO at UCLA Medical Centers

The University of California at Los Angeles agrees to remove the side letter requiring UPTE
employees who are employed at the UCLA Medical Centers participate in the PTO plan at
UCLA. Within ninety (90) days after receipt of written ratification of the HX successor
bargaining agreement, UCLA shall process the employees into the Sick Leave and Vacation
articles of the HX agreement; respectively Articles 36 and 42. This agreement is subject to
the following conditions:

        1. The employees shall be converted to sick leave and vacation in accordance with
           the process(es) utilized by UCLA,

        2. Any employee whose vacation accrual is equal to or more than the maximum
           accrual hours allowed by Article 42, shall receive a payout a payout resulting in
           their balance being at eighty (80%) percent of the allowable accrual under
           Article 42, AND

        3. Employees who are converted from PTO are subject to all terms and conditions
           under Article 36 and 42.
